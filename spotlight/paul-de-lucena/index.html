Why Hire Paul?

Because Paul is hard to fit into one box on a résumé.

His portfolio makes that pretty clear. He’s worked across product definition, architecture, solution design, and technically complex systems, from Contactless Delivery and Owning ETAs to some of the earliest commercial implementations of Learning Tools Interoperability (LTI) at Blackboard.

He can identify a market need, understand the end user, translate that into product and technical requirements… and then actually help build the thing.

Paul moves comfortably between strategy and technical implementation. But what stands out most to me is that he genuinely cares about who the technology is supposed to help.

Ideally, Paul wants to work on technology with social impact. That doesn’t necessarily mean a nonprofit. Plenty of companies are building products that meaningfully improve people’s lives.

Also, somehow, while doing all of this, he still has time for art, photography, ceramics, pasta… I genuinely do not understand his resource allocation.

Plenty of people can say they want to make an impact. Not many can casually say: “Here’s a childhood picture of me with Mother Teresa.” 😂

If you’re hiring a Product Manager, Technical Product Manager, Solutions Architect, or technical product leader who can bridge the gap between high-level strategy and technical implementation, you should meet Paul.
