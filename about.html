About the network

A community-built talent directory.

Talent Network HQ operates a community-built talent directory designed to help experienced professionals become easier to discover. The current cohort consists of Ex-GH talent who have opted in to participate.

Origin

Why This Exists

The directory started as an opt-in spreadsheet created after the Ex-GH layoffs to help impacted employees stay visible to recruiters and hiring teams. As participation grew, the spreadsheet evolved into a searchable talent directory.

Reduce noise. Increase signal.

Process

How It Works

  • Profiles are submitted voluntarily
  • Each submission is reviewed before publishing
  • Only publicly shareable profile information is included
  • Profiles can be updated or removed upon request
  • Placement history is used to understand network outcomes

Governance

Independence & Privacy
