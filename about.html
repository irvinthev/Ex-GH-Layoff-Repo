Why This Exists

It started as a spreadsheet.

After the Grubhub layoffs, a few of us started tracking former teammates who were looking for work. Names turned into rows. Rows turned into patterns.

Pretty quickly, it became clear this was not just a list.

We were organizing signal.

What began as a Google Sheet tracking roughly 300 impacted employees evolved into a searchable talent network designed to make it easier for recruiters to find strong people and easier for candidates to get seen.

Make it easier to find great people, faster.

Not by increasing volume. By increasing clarity.

A Note From Irvin

I spent years at Grubhub, and many of the people in this network are former teammates, colleagues, and friends.

When the layoffs happened, I was navigating my own job search while also watching talented people I knew get reduced to résumés in increasingly noisy applicant pools.

My instinct was to start organizing.

Who was still looking? Who had landed? Where were people going? Who could make an introduction?

I work in technology, audit, risk, and systems, so I tend to look at problems through the lens of structure, signal, and process.

This project became a practical version of that.

Organize the information. Reduce the friction. Help people connect.

The goal was never to build another job board.

It was to make this community easier to see.

What We're Building

The Ex-GH Talent Network is a curated alumni talent network built around two simple problems.

For recruiters: too many applicants, too little signal.

For candidates: too much application volume, too little visibility.

The network sits between those two problems.

It helps recruiters find former Grubhub professionals who are actively exploring opportunities, while giving candidates another way to be discovered outside the traditional application funnel.

How It Works

  • Profiles are submitted voluntarily
  • Each submission is reviewed before publishing
  • Only publicly shareable information, such as LinkedIn, is included
  • Profiles can be updated or removed at any time
  • Placement history is used to understand network outcomes and destination trends

Simple by design. Intentional by default.
