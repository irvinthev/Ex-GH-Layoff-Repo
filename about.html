What This Is

TalentBot HQ is a community-built talent network designed to make experienced professionals easier to discover and connect with new opportunities. The current directory supports Ex-GH talent who have opted in to be listed.

Why This Exists

The directory started as an opt-in spreadsheet created after the Grubhub layoffs to help impacted employees stay visible to recruiters and hiring teams. As participation grew, the spreadsheet evolved into a searchable talent directory.

The goal is simple: reduce noise, increase signal, and make it easier for relevant people to find one another.

How It Works

  • Profiles are submitted voluntarily
  • Each submission is reviewed before publishing
  • Only publicly shareable profile information is included
  • Profiles can be updated or removed at any time
  • Placement history is used to understand network outcomes and destination trends

Simple by design. Intentional by default.
