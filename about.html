Why This Exists

After the Grubhub layoffs, Irvin Thevaranjan created an opt-in, publicly shared spreadsheet to help former teammates looking for work. As the spreadsheet grew and circulated, recruiters started noticing, introductions started happening, and it eventually evolved into this searchable talent directory.

This is an independent, community-built effort—not a Grubhub-sponsored program or recruiting company. Everyone listed has opted in to participate, and the goal is simply to make impacted professionals easier to find and connect with new opportunities.

How It Works

  • Profiles are submitted voluntarily
  • Each submission is reviewed before publishing
  • Only publicly shareable information, such as LinkedIn, is included
  • Profiles can be updated or removed at any time
  • Placement history is used to understand network outcomes and destination trends
